About

I'm a Laravel developer in Dhaka. Most of my work is on the backend: data models, business rules, APIs, queues, and the servers they run on.

At Dingi Dev I build a shift scheduling system for clients in Europe. It plans shifts across timezones and works out leave, overtime and pay using each country's holiday rules. Before that I built a POS and inventory system for an Australian retailer, and the tender search at BdTender, where I started out in support in 2018.

Lately I've been building AI agents and n8n automations on top of Laravel apps. I'm open to remote, freelance or part-time work, especially with teams that need someone to own the backend.

What clients say

Questions people ask

Do you work on fixed-price projects?

Yes, when the scope is clear. I'll ask a few questions first so we agree on what's included before I give a price.

How long does a typical Laravel project take?

It depends on the scope. A simple REST API with auth takes 1–2 weeks. A full SaaS with multi-tenancy and CI/CD can take 6–12 weeks. I'll give you a realistic timeline after the first call.

Are you available for part-time or ongoing work?

Yes. I take 2–3 clients at a time and offer monthly plans for backend support, new features and server maintenance.

Can you add AI features to my existing Laravel app?

Yes. I can add the OpenAI API, a chatbot that answers from your documents, or n8n automations without rewriting your app.

How do you handle communication?

Mostly async on WhatsApp, email or Slack, with a call once a week. I push code often, so you can always see what's done.
