FAQ

Questions people ask

If yours isn't here, just ask.

Do you work on fixed-price projects?
Yes, when the scope is clear. I'll ask a few questions first so we agree on what's included before I give a price.
How long does a typical Laravel project take?
It depends on the scope. A simple REST API with auth takes 1–2 weeks. A full SaaS with multi-tenancy and CI/CD can take 6–12 weeks. I'll give you a realistic timeline after the first call.
Are you available for part-time or ongoing retainer work?
Yes. I currently take 2–3 clients at a time and offer monthly retainer plans for ongoing backend support, feature additions, and server maintenance.
Can you also build AI features into my existing Laravel app?
Yes. I can add the OpenAI API, a chatbot that answers from your documents, or n8n automations to your existing Laravel app without rewriting it.
How do you handle communication during a project?
Mostly async on WhatsApp, email or Slack, with a call once a week. I push code often, so you can always see what's done.
